COUNTRY: Ecuador
PERIOD: Jun 2024 / Mar 2025
========================================

The first IPC Acute Food Insecurity analysis in Ecuador has covered all 24 provinces, revealing that approximately 2.7 million people are expected to face high levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 or higher) during the projection period (September 2024 - March 2025). These people will require urgent intervention, especially those households that depend on the sale of labour, mainly linked to the agricultural sector, and face depletion of food stocks and deprivation of essential needs such as water and basic sanitation.
The food insecurity situation is due to key factors such as high food prices, violence and insecurity, low household incomes and unemployment.
